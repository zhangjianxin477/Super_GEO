## Generic enterprise policy

The registry describes an initial validation configuration, not a mandatory channel set. Any enterprise may add or remove channels through its approved Market Pack, with an accountable owner, documented editorial constraints, and a reviewed distribution path.

## Completion evidence

A task is not completed merely because content was prepared. Completion requires a workspace-scoped proof artifact—such as a permitted published URL, channel confirmation, or submission record—and remains an observed action, not proof of future AI-platform visibility.
